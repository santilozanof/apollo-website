# Music recap redesign candidate — 2026-10-28

This is a local release candidate on top of `7428077e1ac14b86e76da2dac80b73490aa03134`. Production has **not** been updated.

The recap view now reads the same period-aware Music Insights summary for weekly and monthly recaps. It keeps the existing monthly archive and refresh route. Three slides cover featured music, the top five tracks with credited artists, and the top five artists. Noir, Atmosphere, Editorial, and After Hours have distinct canvas compositions and can export Story (1080 × 1920), portrait (1080 × 1350), or square (1080 × 1080). The PNG and ZIP exports use the same renderer as the preview. Art URLs come from exact track or artist Spotify metadata. Name-only exports can borrow an image from a later identified play only when the exact names and, for tracks, album match and no conflicting Spotify ID exists. A missing, failed or ambiguous image uses a typographic fallback. The artwork palette colors the ambient themes. Listening time is labeled verified or estimated, never inferred from full track length.

Synthetic local browser QA covered a populated monthly recap, a populated week and an empty week. Noir, Editorial and After Hours were visually inspected at Story and square sizes; square-cover overlap found in the first pass was corrected. The downloaded After Hours square PNG was inspected at exactly 1080 × 1080. The renderer test covers all four themes, three formats, three slides, matched image IDs, five rank entries and long-title truncation. A backend test covers exact unambiguous art reuse and rejects same-name Spotify ID collisions. Python suite: 368 tests, 366 pass and two existing optional skips. iOS/JavaScript suite: 16 pass. Production asset build, JavaScript syntax and Git whitespace checks passed.

This fixture has no Spotify IDs or artwork, so its visual result exercises fallbacks.
